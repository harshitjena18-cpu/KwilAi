## 2026-08-29 - ARIA labels for Icon-Only Buttons
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons (common in utility bars like header/footer actions) often lack explicit s, creating an accessibility blind spot for screen reader users.
**Action:** When adding or modifying utility buttons with only icons (like Share, Download, Back, Trash), explicitly verify and add s to ensure accessibility is maintained.
